How This Works
A quick tour of the live scorecard.

Entering scores

Tap a hole's box and type the gross score — the cursor jumps to the next box automatically so you can keep both thumbs moving down the card. Every save shows a brief "Saving…/Saved" flash near the top.

Undo

Changed a score by mistake? A gold "Undo" button appears bottom-right for a few seconds after every entry — tap it to instantly restore the previous value.

Day 1 · Day 2 · Day 3 tabs

Each day scores differently — Day 1 is head-to-head match play, Day 2 is a team scramble, Day 3 is individual Stableford. Switch tabs any time; everyone's device stays in sync automatically every 30 seconds, or tap 🔄 Refresh for an instant update.

Teams tab

On the morning of Day 1, the two captains alternate picks until all fourteen players are assigned — do that here. Once teams are set, every other tab already knows who's on which side.

Getting stuck?

Nothing you enter here is ever truly lost — every score is logged with who entered it and when (tap any score to see it), and an organiser can restore an earlier value from the Admin tab's Field History.

Team Scramble
Each team splits into a group of 4 and a group of 3. Enter each group's net score to par (e.g. -15 for 15 under). Scoring: in each grouping (4v4 and 3v3) the team with the better score wins points equal to the stroke differential — e.g. -15 beats -12 for 3pts. Whichever team's combined 4+3 score is better also takes a flat 5pt bonus.
