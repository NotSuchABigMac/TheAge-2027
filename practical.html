01 Kit List What to Bring

Pack like an adult. Play like a child.

August in Yarrawonga: frost at sunrise, low twenties by lunch, a chance of a westerly off the lake. Pack for all three.

The Obvious

  • Clubs. A full set. Borrowing a wedge mid-round counts as a meltdown moment.
  • Balls. A dozen minimum. Two dozen if you play the lake holes the way you usually do.
  • Tees. Bring twice what you think you need.
  • Glove. Two, if there's any rain in the forecast.
  • Shoes. Spikeless or soft spikes. Pro shop is fussy.

On Course

  • Collared shirts × 3. Clubhouse rules. No singlets, no exceptions.
  • Rain jacket. It's August. Don't be the bloke borrowing one.
  • Layers. A puffer or vest for the early tee.
  • Hat. Cap or bucket. The Goose Hat is issued separately.
  • Sunscreen. Yes, even in August. UV doesn't read calendars.
  • Towel. For clubs. Not for crying.

Accommodation

  • Sheets & pillowcases. Not provided - pack accordingly or air-dry.
  • Towels. Bath towels not provided.
  • Food & drinks. For the house - kitchen & BBQ are available.
  • Toiletries. All the usual suspects.
  • Warm layers. Yarrawonga evenings get cold in August.

Wallet

  • $200 cash. $100 for skins & side bets. $100 for the pub.
  • Card. Accommodation, dinners, green fees, that round you owe Hughesy.
  • Medicare card. Optimistic. Possible.
