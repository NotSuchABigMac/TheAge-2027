How the draft works

Instead of preset teams, captains are chosen by the field on the Friday morning — whoever the group votes for leads a side. The two captains then alternate picks in a snake draft until all 14 players are allocated.

  1. Peer vote for captains Friday morning, before tee-off — players nominate and vote for two captains.
  2. Snake draft Captains alternate picks (1-2-2-2-…-1) until all 14 players are assigned to a team.
  3. Team names chosen by captains Each captain names their side — bragging rights on the line from the first pick.
  4. Live scorecard tracks the field Open now — shows the draft in progress before tee-off, then every match as it's played.

Three days of competition follow: Friday match play, Saturday scramble, Sunday Stableford. Points accumulate across all three rounds — the team with the most points lifts the cup.

How the Cup is scored

Three days, three different formats, one running points total. Here's exactly how each day pays points, plus the bonus and house rules that apply on top.

Day I · Friday

Singles Match Play Murray Course

Twelve players (Cayden Woods & Ben Lepore join the field Saturday) across six 1v1 singles matches. Each match splits into a front 9 and a back 9, each worth 1 point to the winning team — 0.5pt each if a nine is halved. 12 points are up for grabs across the six matches.

Handicap strokes are allocated by the standard difference-in-handicap method: the lower-handicap player plays off scratch, and the higher-handicap player receives one stroke on every hole — plus a second stroke on the lowest-stroke-index holes — for as many holes as the rounded handicap difference between the two players requires.

Day II · Saturday

Team Scramble Black Bull

Each team splits into a group of 4 and a group of 3, each playing a scramble and returning one net score to par per group. The group's team handicap is worked out from its players' individual handicaps using the standard Australian Ambrose percentages — 25/20/15/10 for a four, 30/20/10 for a three — applied lowest-handicap-first.

In each grouping — the two 4s against each other, the two 3s against each other — the team with the better net score wins points equal to the stroke differential between the two results. Whichever team's combined 4 + 3 score is better also takes a flat 5-point bonus.

House rule · National Anthem

Every player's own scramble group score is adjusted individually depending on how they handled the national anthem before Saturday's round kicks off: +2 strokes if you didn't sing it, −1 stroke if you did. It's added straight to the group's score per player — not to anyone's handicap, and not a flat, team-wide adjustment.

Day III · Sunday

Individual Net Stableford Lake Course

Bonus · Every Day

Nearest the Pin

Two par-3 holes each day carry a Nearest the Pin bonus, worth 1 point to the winner's team — 6 points up for grabs across the weekend on top of the day scores above:

  • Day I, Murray Course — Hole 8 & Hole 17
  • Day II, Black Bull — Hole 4 & Hole 16
  • Day III, Lake Course — Hole 7 & Hole 14
The Cup

Overall Result

Points from all three days — match play, scramble, and Stableford, plus every Nearest the Pin bonus — add up to one running total per team, tracked live on the live scorecard. Whichever team has more points after Sunday's presentation wins the Wonga Cup. Level on points after all three days? It's settled by a sudden-death putt-off on the spot.
