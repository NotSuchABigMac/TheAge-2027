How the draft works

Instead of preset teams, captains are chosen by the field on the Friday morning — whoever the group votes for leads a side. The two captains then alternate picks in a snake draft until all 14 players are allocated.

  1. Peer vote for captains Friday morning, before tee-off — players nominate and vote for two captains.
  2. Snake draft Captains alternate picks (1-2-2-2-…-1) until all 14 players are assigned to a team.
  3. Team names chosen by captains Each captain names their side — bragging rights on the line from the first pick.
  4. Live scorecard goes live Once teams are confirmed, this page will link through to the full scoring tool.

Three days of competition follow: Friday match play, Saturday scramble, Sunday Stableford. Points accumulate across all three rounds — the team with the most points lifts the cup.

The live scoring tool will be available once the draft is complete on Friday morning.
