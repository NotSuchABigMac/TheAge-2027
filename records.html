02 Trophies On the Line

One Cup. Six side honours.

The Cup is contested between sides — each edition gives it a new name, and 2026's is the Wonga Cup. The six minor honours are decided across individuals, hole records, and the verdict of a Pub Captain whose tenure is yet to be challenged.

Cup The Wonga Cup

Awarded to the winning side across all three days. Bragging rights until the following August. Named fresh each edition.
